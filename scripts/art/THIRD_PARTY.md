# Classic pointer reference

The 11 × 19 arrow bitmap in `cursors.mjs` follows JS Paint's classic pointer.
It is stamped at 1,1 in a transparent 32 × 32 canvas, with the hotspot at its tip.

Source: https://github.com/1j01/jspaint/blob/master/images/cursors/default.png
Git blob: `60883d9e8e0664113696c4a450779f46adf21b9a`
